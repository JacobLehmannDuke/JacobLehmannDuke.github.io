Joint work introducing topological methods for zero-sum problems building on the Erdős-Ginzburg-Ziv theorem, including a topological criterion for zero-sum hyperedges in ℤ/n-colorings of n-uniform hypergraphs, a fractional generalization of the EGZ theorem, and a constrained EGZ theorem restricting the combinatorics of zero-sum sequences.
